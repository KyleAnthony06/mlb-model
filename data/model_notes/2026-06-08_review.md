## Repeatable lessons

1. Full-slate first-inning leans should be informational only. Do not treat every full-slate lean as a tracked best bet unless the confidence clears a threshold.
2. For official tracked picks, raise the first-inning threshold: prefer 58%+ for NRFI/YRFI unless the user explicitly asks to track all full-slate leans.
3. Hitter total bases should be concentrated in the very best hitting environments or with strong platoon/order edges. Do not spread hitter props broadly just because a hitter is elite.
4. Pitcher K unders against durable starters remain dangerous. Avoid unders unless there is a strong pitch-count, role, or matchup constraint.
5. Parlay legs should come from the highest-confidence subset only; avoid using a hitter prop at 62% if it is not supported by park/team context as strongly as the MIL @ ATH plays were.

## Rule changes for next model version

- Separate `FULL_SLATE_LEAN` from official tracked bet categories, or track only best bets above threshold.
- Official first-inning bet threshold: 58%+ preferred; 60%+ for parlay consideration.
- Hitter props: prioritize environment/team context over star name; top hitter prop must have both individual skill and game environment support.
- Pitcher props: avoid K unders on proven workload arms; use fewer K props overall until tracked performance improves.
- Two-leg parlay: choose the two strongest independent edges, with at least one leg from the model's best-performing sleeve and both at 60%+ confidence.
