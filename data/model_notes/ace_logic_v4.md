## v4 rules

### NRFI

1. Keep cards selective. Do not force a Top 10.
2. Require two-sided starter viability plus a non-dangerous first-inning environment.
3. Apply extra penalty for high totals, heat, wind out, Coors/Sutter, or repeated first-inning damage from similar top orders.
4. Closed roof/dome is neutral weather control, not an automatic boost.
5. Avoid games where both top threes have multiple OBP/power threats unless both starters are strong.

### Pitcher props

1. K overs need one of two profiles:
   - Elite whiff pitcher with normal workload, or
   - Discounted line with strong workload proof and opponent K path.
2. Avoid mid-tier K overs that rely only on the opponent being strikeout-prone.
3. K unders require a bigger edge after May 14. Avoid under 3.5 unless a pitch-count/opener risk exists.
4. Unders against pitchers with normal leash should generally require a high line, contact-heavy opponent, and weak pitcher whiff profile.

## May 15 implementation

The May 15 card emphasizes NRFI spots with low totals/controlled environments and K overs for premium or stable workload arms. K unders are mostly avoided because May 14 showed poor margin for error on unders without a strong workload cap.
