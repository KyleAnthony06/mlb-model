# Ace Logic v5 Model Notes

This version incorporates May 12-15 graded results.

## Performance baseline

- Aggregate: 26-20-1, 56.5%
- NRFI/YRFI: 14-8, 63.6%
- Props overall: 12-12-1, 50.0%
- Hitter props first sample: 3-2

## v5 rules

### Full-slate first-inning model

- Provide a first-inning lean for every game when the user asks for full slate.
- Track confidence honestly; low-confidence YRFI/NRFI leans are not equal to official best bets.
- Strongest inputs remain: two-sided starter viability, park/weather, top-three OBP/power, and game total.
- Hitter-friendly environments are often YRFI or hitter-prop targets, not NRFI targets.

### Pitcher props

- Use fewer pitcher K props.
- Confirm starter role where possible.
- Prefer elite whiff arms with stable workload or discounted lines with clear workload support.
- Avoid relying only on opponent strikeout reputation.

### Hitter props

- Total bases are preferred over HR props.
- Target high-total parks, heat, wind out, weak contact pitchers, platoon advantages, and top-half batting order.
- HR props remain longshot/value-only.

### Parlay construction

- Two-leg parlays should use independent games/markets where possible.
- Prefer one NRFI/YRFI leg from the strongest first-inning edge plus one hitter/pitcher prop from a separate game.
- Do not stack multiple fragile pitcher props in a parlay.
