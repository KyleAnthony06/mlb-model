## Repeatable lessons for June 8 and later

1. Keep NRFI/YRFI as the primary slate-level model sleeve.
2. Add hitter total bases as a regular secondary sleeve; prioritize park, weather, batting order, platoon, and total.
3. Keep HR props rare and lower confidence even when projected EV looks positive.
4. Tighten pitcher props further: require confirmed starter status, strong workload expectation, and either elite whiff edge or discounted line.
5. For parlay construction, combine independent legs from the strongest sleeves rather than stacking correlated high-variance props.
