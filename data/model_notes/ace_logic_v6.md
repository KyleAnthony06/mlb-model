# Ace Logic v6 Model Notes

This version incorporates May 12-15 and June 8 grading.

## Performance baseline

- Aggregate: 32-31-1, 50.8%
- NRFI/YRFI: 17-13, 56.7%
- Props overall: 15-18-1, 45.5%

## Major update from June 8

June 8 exposed a difference between "full-slate lean" and "official bet." Full-slate informational leans should not all be treated as equal best bets. Low-confidence leans in the 53-56% range performed poorly.

## v6 rules

### Slate output

- If the user asks for full slate, provide a lean for every game but label it as informational unless it clears the official threshold.
- Official tracked first-inning picks should generally be 58%+.
- Parlay consideration should generally be 60%+ and from independent games/markets.

### NRFI/YRFI

- Keep prioritizing park/weather and two-sided starter profile.
- Avoid using top-order names alone as a YRFI signal when the pitcher/park context is run-suppressing.
- High-run environments can still be YRFI targets, but only when the park/weather/game total signal is strong.

### Pitcher props

- Reduce volume until performance improves.
- Avoid K unders on durable starters without pitch-count or role constraints.
- K overs need line value plus workload plus matchup; one factor is not enough.

### Hitter props

- Hitter total bases remain viable but must be concentrated in elite hitting contexts or clear platoon/order edges.
- Star power alone is not enough for O 1.5 TB; Yordan and Vlad both missed without enough game-environment support.
- HR props remain longshot-only and should be separated from core plays.

### Parlays

- Use only 60%+ legs where possible.
- Prefer one strong first-inning edge plus one hitter prop from an elite run environment, or two independent best bets.
- Keep parlay stake smaller than straight bets.
